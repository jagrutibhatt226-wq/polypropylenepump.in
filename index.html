07 FAQ

PP pump questions, answered.

What is a polypropylene pump?

A polypropylene pump is a corrosion-resistant centrifugal pump with polypropylene wetted construction for compatible acids, alkalis, chemicals and industrial process fluids.

What is a PP acid pump used for?

PP acid pumps are used for compatible acid transfer, pickling, effluent treatment, water treatment, chemical processing and other corrosive-fluid applications.

What is a magnetic drive polypropylene pump?

It is a sealless centrifugal pump in which magnetic coupling transfers torque from the motor side to the impeller side without a conventional mechanical shaft seal.

What materials are available?

Depending on the model and application, polypropylene, PVDF, UHMW-type materials and selected shaft, bearing and sealing materials can be considered.

How do I select the right PP chemical pump?

Provide the liquid, concentration, temperature, flow, total head, suction conditions, viscosity, solids content and preferred material. These details help determine the suitable configuration.
